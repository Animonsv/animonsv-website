# ANIMONSV — Technology & Electronics

**Technology That Moves You Forward.**

A premium, responsive technology brand website built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Pages

- `index.html` — Home
- `products.html` — Searchable and filterable product concept catalog
- `categories.html` — Technology categories
- `innovations.html` — Innovation philosophy
- `about.html` — Brand story
- `contact.html` — Demonstration contact form
- `faq.html` — Frequently asked questions
- `privacy.html` — Privacy notice
- `terms.html` — Terms of use

## Brand identity

- Midnight Navy: `#0F172A`
- Electric Blue: `#3B82F6`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Language: English
- Style: Modern & Professional

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser or run `python -m http.server 8000` and visit `http://localhost:8000`.

## Deploy on GitHub Pages

Upload the **contents** of this folder to the root of the `Animonsv/animonsv-website` repository. In repository Settings → Pages choose Deploy from a branch → `main` → `/ (root)` and Save. The planned URL is `https://animonsv.github.io/animonsv-website/`.

## Important

This is a **demonstration website**, not an operational store. Products and prices are illustrative. No purchases or payments are processed, and the contact form does not transmit messages. Photos and fonts are loaded from external services and require an internet connection. Replace placeholders and review legal notices before a real launch.
